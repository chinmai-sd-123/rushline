# Ticketing and refunds

## Holds
Selected seats are held for you for 10 minutes while you pay. If you don't pay in time, the hold expires and the seats go back on sale. You can hold at most 6 seats per show, and only one hold per show at a time.

## Payment
We authorize your card first and only capture the money once your booking is saved. If anything fails before capture, the authorization is voided and no money moves.

## Cancellations
You can cancel a confirmed booking from your account at any time before the show. The seats go back on sale immediately.

## Refunds
Cancelled bookings are not refunded automatically. For a refund, contact support with your booking id within 7 days of cancelling. Refunds are not available for shows that have already started.
