# Entry and security

## Doors
Doors open 45 minutes before the show starts. Late entry is at the venue's discretion and may wait for a suitable break.

## ID and tickets
Bring the booking confirmation on your phone. Tickets are personal: the name on the booking must match a photo ID for shows marked "named tickets".

## Bags
Small bags up to 30 x 20 x 10 cm are allowed. Large bags, backpacks and luggage are not allowed and there is no cloakroom.

## Prohibited items
Outside food and drink, professional cameras, laser pointers and any kind of weapon are not allowed inside the venue.
